EP’s rich personas reproduced the purchase-intent spread. But subgroup errors canceled: Qwen was less enthusiastic among adventurous consumers and more willing among mainstream consumers.

Key result: Persona design explains much of the polarization; model + image + elicitation still matter. Synthetic hypotheses—not population estimates.
